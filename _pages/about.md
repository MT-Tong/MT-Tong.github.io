Welcome! I am a PhD candidate in Economics at McGill University in Canada.

***I am on the 2026–2027 Job Market!***

My research interests lie at the intersection of **econometrics, financial economics, and macroeconomics**. I develop econometric methods that use financial-market data to study financial risks and economic shocks that are not directly observed. My work focuses on how financial risk evolves, how different shocks shape market reactions, and how their effects propagate through the broader economy. These methods support more reliable risk measurement, forecasting, and macroeconomic policy evaluation.
